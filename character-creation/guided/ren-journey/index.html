Guided Walkthrough: Your Ren Journey

This section covers the arc of your character’s relationship with Ren, from the first moment they sensed it to how they learned to wield it, which faction they ended up attached to, and where they stand in the world now.

These results are some of the most important for understanding your character’s motivations. Pay attention to what comes up.

Sensing Ren

Section titled “Sensing Ren”

At Realm 1, you’ve sensed Ren. This is always a response to an extreme emotional or physical state. Roll d12.

d12Moment
1Overwhelming grief
2Blinding anger
3Terror
4Protective instinct
5Crushing shame
6Ecstatic joy
7Deep isolation
8Moral conflict
9Physical exhaustion
10Betrayal
11Awe
12Quiet realization

Think about what that moment meant for your character. Who was involved? What were the stakes? This is one of the defining memories of their life.

Why You Learned Ren

Section titled “Why You Learned Ren”

Sensing Ren is involuntary. Pursuing it is a choice, and rarely an easy one. Roll d20.

d20Origin
1You were going to die if you didn’t
2Someone you loved was harmed
3You were powerless and humiliated
4Your family expected it
5You needed answers no one could give
6A mentor chose you
7You sought out a master
8It awakened by accident
9You were forced into training
10You needed to escape control
11Others depended on you
12Religious revelation or calling
13A rival pushed you
14You caused harm and swore control
15You were weak or sickly
16You wanted recognition
17You needed to fix something broken
18Inheritance expected it
19You pursued it for the experience
20Ren reacted uniquely to you

How You Learned Ren

Section titled “How You Learned Ren”

Once you had a reason, someone or something had to teach you. Roll d20.

d20Teacher
1–8Faction group training
9–12Military or government training
13–16Direct apprenticeship or specialized training
17–18Religious discipline or academic study
19Forbidden / stolen / found texts
20Survival necessity, on your own

Write down any relationships that came from this period: a mentor, a fellow student, a rival from training. Roll their status later in Who You Are Now.

Faction

Section titled “Faction”

Learning Ren almost always comes with strings. Very few Rennim operate without any institutional attachment. Roll d100.

d100Faction
01–18Lesser Guild
19–40Eventide Brigade
41–58Government (Imperial Dynasty, Sand Swept Princes, Tribal Council, Hammer Accord, The Assembly, or a Highborn House)
59–73Ren Sect (Frozen Flower / Maelstrom / Daemon / Celestial Sword)
74–79The Gate of Zerathrain
80–85Temple Religion
86–90The Black Tower
91–94Yrialin
95–96Yliasen
97–98The Silver Hand
99None
100Cult (Black Chalice / Awoken / Thousand Tongues)

Then roll d8. On a 1–7, the faction that taught you Ren is the same faction you’re working for now. On an 8, they’re different. Reroll the faction table for your current affiliation.

Add the faction(s) to your Relationships section.

See Factions for full descriptions of each.

Ren Texture

Section titled “Ren Texture”

Ren Texture describes how your Ren circulates. It determines when boons and complications trigger on a roll. Roll d6.

d6TextureTriggers
1–2TranquilMin and Max values only
3–4VolatileMin, Min+1 and Max-1, Max
5–6RampantMin, Min+1, Min+2 and Max-2, Max-1, Max

Tranquil is stable: fewer surprises in either direction. Rampant is swingy: more chances for things to go spectacularly right or badly wrong. Think about which fits your character’s relationship with their power.

See Ren Texture for the full mechanical breakdown.

World Standing

Section titled “World Standing”

Now that you have a faction, roll d20 to determine your current standing within that structure and the world at large.

d20Position
1Hidden (existence intentionally concealed)
2Tolerated / Hunted
3Protected (a faction shelters you)
4Sponsored (support with expectation of return)
5Watched (a faction monitors you)
6–8Independent (no faction, no protection)
9–11Affiliated (cooperative, no obligation)
12–14Agent (sent to act on faction’s behalf)
15–17Bound (you owe a debt and cannot freely leave)
18–20Member (you belong, operate under rules)

Most characters will be Members or Bound. If you rolled Independent but also have a faction, you’re in a liminal position worth exploring.
