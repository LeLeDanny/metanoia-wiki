Upbringing & Family

Environment You Grew Up In

Section titled “Environment You Grew Up In”

Roll d20 for the scale and type of settlement.

d20Result
1-2Hamlet / Cabin / Wilderness
3-6Village
7-13Town
14-18City
19-20Fortress / Encampment

Feel free to invent a name for the place. Don’t change the capital cities of each nation, but everything else is yours to name.

Texture

Section titled “Texture”

Texture is a measure of how much your Ren does things on its own. The more it does, the harder it is for it to be precise, but it can have greater impact. The less it acts out, the easier it is for it to be precise.

d6TextureWhat it means
1-2RampantYour ren acts out on its own a lot but is harder to be precise with, but yields greater effect
3-4VolatileYour ren acts out here and there and when it does it has a little stronger effect than tranquil
5-6TranquilYour ren doesn’t act out on its own and is extremely precise

  2. Their relationship with you
  3. Parent’s Background

Upbringing

Section titled “Upbringing”

Who Actually Raised You

Section titled “Who Actually Raised You”
d6Who Raised YouExample
1Parent(s)Raised by parent(s), whatever their Status turned out to be.
2Close FamilyA grandparent, aunt, or uncle took you in when your parents couldn’t.
3Older SiblingA brother or sister barely grown themselves became the one who fed you.
4Adoptive FamilyStrangers by blood who chose you, by custom, contract, or kindness.
5Guardian / MentorA family friend, tutor, or hired hand ended up the one who actually raised you.
6InstitutionAn orphanage, workhouse, guild, temple, religious order, or similar, raised alongside dozens of others like you.

Their Style of Raising You

Section titled “Their Style of Raising You”

Roll d20 for how the person or people who raised you actually treated you day to day. Write an experience for this.

d20StyleExample
1Nurturing & AttunedWarm and responsive, they noticed when something was wrong before you said a word.
2Encouraging & Hands-offLet you fail on your own terms, cheered loudest after you’d already picked yourself up.
3Strict but FairRules were firm but explained, punishments matched the offense.
4Helicopter / OverprotectiveHovered over every choice, rarely let you cross a room without checking in.
5Permissive / IndulgentSaid yes to avoid a fight, gave you what you wanted rather than what you needed.
6Enmeshed / Living Through YouYour wins were theirs, your failures felt like theirs too, there was no daylight between you.
7Authoritarian / ControllingObedience first, questions never, the rules didn’t need a reason.
8Conditional LoveAffection arrived after good grades or good behavior, and dried up without them.
9Emotionally NeglectfulFed and housed, never comforted, you learned not to bring your problems home.
10Physically NeglectfulMeals, clothes, and attention were inconsistent, you filled the gaps yourself early.
11Inconsistent / ErraticWarm and doting one day, cold or explosive the next, you learned to read the room before speaking.
12Manipulative / Guilt-DrivenDebts and obligations were the currency, “after everything I’ve done for you” was a constant refrain.
13Verbally AbusiveCriticism and insults were the default tone, praise was rare enough to remember exactly when it happened.
14Physically AbusiveDiscipline meant hitting, and it didn’t always wait for a reason.
15Psychologically AbusiveGaslighting and isolation, your version of events was never the one that counted.
16Militaristic / RegimentedThe household ran on a schedule with punishment built into it, missteps were logged, not forgiven.
17Dogmatic / DoctrinaireRaised strictly by a faith or code, deviation was treated as a moral failure.
18Absent ProviderMoney and shelter were never in question, their presence in your life was.
19Best Friend, No AuthorityTreated as an equal or a confidant, structure and guidance rarely showed up.
20Used YouValued for the labor, coin, or status you brought in more than for who you were.
