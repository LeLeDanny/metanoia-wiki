Keywords

The Core Mechanical concept

Section titled “The Core Mechanical concept”

Metanoia is a game mechanized on Language (words and clauses), applied to anything. We call this construct a tag. These are presented in a two main categories:

  1. Pre-Defined
  2. Un-Defined

Pre-Defined language has meaning within the system and can’t be overwritten. Un-Defined meas that it’s up to the players and the GM to determine what the language means in the rules.

Functional Classifications of Pre-Defined Individual Words

Section titled “Functional Classifications of Pre-Defined Individual Words”
  • Polarity = A word that has associated keyword(s)
  • Keyword = A word that has a distinct effect

Terminology for types of Keywords

Section titled “Terminology for types of Keywords”
LayerWhat it is
ConditionA keyword that performs an effect on a character without inflicting an injury
PassiveA keyword that is always on for the character unless they decide to deactivate it - can be reactivated
EscalatorA keyword that automatically repeats a minimum effect on a tick while it remains active. Which minimum effect it uses is determined by its Injury field - if Injury is true, then it adds an injury every other round.

Every keyword also carries an Injury field (True/Choice/False). If a keyword’s Injury field is True, applying it fills one of the target’s 3 Injury slots directly. Some keywords automatically cause Injurires (T), some don’t (F), and with others, you can choose (C).

Perversion (Damnation’s Keyword)

Section titled “Perversion (Damnation’s Keyword)”

Everyone is Corrupted by default. Damnation only comes through choosing Perversion, and there is no turning back once you have.

Perversion is earned by choosing atrocity for power. It mirrors the structure of Realm advancement: each rank is a structural shift in who you are, and unlike normal Keywords its Rank is not capped at 5. It can be granted to your character as an extra keyword, even if you have the ‘maximum’.

Each tier requires a sustained pattern of the described behavior, not a single act. The Threshold Act is the deed that finally locks a tier in once the pattern is established, the same way a Realm breakthrough needs both practice and a moment of exposure.

While Perversion is active, its Rank is added to every Keyword you use.

Ren Texture Keywords

Section titled “Ren Texture Keywords”

This shows the table of the expected successes from a d6.

Texture is a measure of how responsive a character’s Ren is to their subconscious - the numbers in the table are the number of successes when you roll that number on each d6 in your dice pool. There are 3 textures, and a character can only have 1, and it doesn’t change.

  • Tranquil - Your Ren is naturally precise, but hesitates or rarely moves without your instruction (consistent but moderate)
  • Volatile - Your Ren can move without your instruction, but doesn’t do it very often. (This is the inbetween texture)
  • Rampant - Your Ren moves without your instruction all the time, but is harder to control. (Swingy but powerful)

Entrenched is defense only so a target can’t restore Conviction on its own turn faster than a frame can remove it.

Shaken is applied by the Hold rule in Social Frames, never cast. Shaken [Motive] and Shaken [Identity] are tracked separately. Neither stacks with itself: a new Shaken of the same type replaces the old one only if its Rank is higher, and its days and invocation count start over.
