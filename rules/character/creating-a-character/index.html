Creating a Character

Character creation in Metanoia is a process of defining identity before stats. The mechanical numbers follow from who your character is and what they believe.

Step 1: Choose Your Lineage

Section titled “Step 1: Choose Your Lineage”

Select one of the seven Thuim lineages. Your lineage determines your biological traits and grants you an inherent polarity tied to one of the Laws of Ren.

See the Lineages section for descriptions of each.

If you are playing a Thylini (mixed-lineage), work with your GM to determine which lineage traits apply.

Step 2: Set Your Realm

Section titled “Step 2: Set Your Realm”

Most starting characters begin at Realm 1 (Spark). Confirm with your GM. Your Realm affects Strain, free dice, favor, and ability costs.

Step 3: Assign Your Values

Section titled “Step 3: Assign Your Values”

Choose 4 values from the baseline list and rank them in priority order (1 = highest, 4 = lowest). Your values define what your character believes and will fight for.

The ranking matters mechanically: value priority determines the die size your associated polarity contributes via the Circulation passive.

See Values for the full list and how values shift during play.

Step 4: Choose Your Polarities

Section titled “Step 4: Choose Your Polarities”

For each value, choose one or more polarities that conceptually fit how your character experiences that value. A polarity must make narrative sense given the value it is tied to.

Your lineage grants one inherent polarity regardless of value selection.

See Polarities for the full taxonomy.

Step 5: Calculate Strain

Section titled “Step 5: Calculate Strain”

Strain = 4 + (2 × (Realm + XP invested in Strain))

At Realm 1 with no XP invested, your starting Strain maximum is 6. The absolute maximum is 24.

See Strain.

Step 6: Determine Stride

Section titled “Step 6: Determine Stride”

Base Stride = 2 + XP invested in Stride

Stride is measured in Range Bands per turn. At no XP investment, your starting Stride is 2.

See Stride.

Step 7: Assign Starting Favor

Section titled “Step 7: Assign Starting Favor”

You receive 2 + Realm Favor points to assign across your starting relationships. You can take -1 on one relationship to gain +1 to spend elsewhere.

See Favor.

Step 8: Choose Active Abilities

Section titled “Step 8: Choose Active Abilities”

You begin with a number of active abilities up to your maximum (Realm × 5). Each ability requires 4 practice sessions to learn, so your starting abilities represent ones your character already knows before the game begins. Work with your GM on what you arrive with.

See Active Abilities for how to build them.

Step 9: Choose Passive Abilities

Section titled “Step 9: Choose Passive Abilities”

Passive abilities each cost 1 XP per level. Circulation is the most important passive for most characters, as it is what allows your polarity dice to contribute to rolls.

See Passive Abilities.

Step 10: Gear and Starting Silver

Section titled “Step 10: Gear and Starting Silver”

Starting silver = 50 × (Realm × 4). At Realm 1, you begin with 200 Ve to spend on equipment.

See Equipment and Currency.
