Polarities

Polarities are the specific flavors of Ren a character can wield. Each polarity must be tied to one of your values. The connection must be conceptually coherent: the polarity should reflect how your character experiences that value.

Your lineage grants one inherent polarity regardless of value selection. All other polarities are chosen at character creation or unlocked through value shifts during play.

There are 110 polarities organized into 13 categories, plus 8 lineage polarities tied to the Laws of Ren.

Using Polarities in Play

Section titled “Using Polarities in Play”

Polarities are always usable as the foundation for active abilities. Without the Circulation passive, that is your only avenue for invoking them mechanically. Circulation is what unlocks adding a polarity die directly to any relevant roll, outside of a specific ability.

The key constraint is conceptual coherence. A polarity can only do what it could plausibly do. Heat polarity sucking warmth out of a room is valid. Heat polarity creating a shadow is not.

See Active Abilities and Passive Abilities.
