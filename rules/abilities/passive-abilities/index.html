Passive Abilities

Passive abilities are permanent upgrades that modify how your character functions. They are purchased with XP at 1 XP per level and do not need to be activated.

Circulation

Section titled “Circulation”

The most important passive for most characters.

Circulation allows you to add a polarity die directly to any roll where that polarity is narratively relevant, outside of a specific active ability. Without Circulation, polarities can only be invoked through active abilities.

The die size contributed is determined by the rank of the polarity’s associated value:

Value RankDie Size
#1 (highest priority)d4
#2d6
#3d8
#4 (lowest priority)d10

Each level of Circulation covers one polarity. To circulate multiple polarities, purchase multiple levels.

Resistant

Section titled “Resistant”

Gain resistance to a specific damage type. Each level reduces incoming Strain of that type by 1.

Can be taken multiple times for different damage types. Each damage type is tracked and leveled separately.

Juggernaut

Section titled “Juggernaut”

Ignore conditions from wounds that dealt only 1 Strain.

Advanced Awareness

Section titled “Advanced Awareness”

One state of your Awareness gains a boost to range. Each level increases how far that state reaches. Can be taken multiple times, either to boost the same state further or to apply to a different state.

Stability

Section titled “Stability”

Each level reduces any forced movement applied to you by 1 Range Band.

Advanced Sense

Section titled “Advanced Sense”

Gain an enhanced or alternative sense: seeing in complete darkness, seeing heat signatures, perceiving through a blindfold, or similar. Choose one specific sense when taking this passive.

Never at Loss

Section titled “Never at Loss”

When you would suffer from any complications, you regain 1 Strain.

Armored Aura

Section titled “Armored Aura”

You have natural armor with a maximum AS equal to the XP invested in this passive. It regenerates up to 2 points every time you sleep.

Freedom of Movement

Section titled “Freedom of Movement”

Ignore terrain effects that reduce your Stride.

Unconstrained Mind

Section titled “Unconstrained Mind”

You are immune to mind control.

Focused Execution

Section titled “Focused Execution”

When your dice pool is built from 3 or fewer sources, you gain +1 success on the roll.

Undetected Awareness

Section titled “Undetected Awareness”

One state of your Awareness is only detectable to those who share a specific polarity you choose at purchase. Can be taken multiple times for different Awareness states or polarities. If taken for overlapping polarities, a detector needs both to perceive you.

Buildup

Section titled “Buildup”

Designate a specific action, interaction, or ability. Each time that trigger occurs, gain an escalating die starting at d12 and improving by one step each time (d12 > d10 > d8 > d6 > d4). The die caps at d4. You can add it to any roll at any time, then it resets.

No Damage, All Condition

Section titled “No Damage, All Condition”

When you successfully wound an enemy with a specific damage type of your choice, you may forgo the Strain damage and apply a condition instead.

Shared Assurance

Section titled “Shared Assurance”

When you and an ally suffer the same specific negative effect simultaneously, you gain +1 success on your next action.

Self-Sustaining Summons

Section titled “Self-Sustaining Summons”

Duration costs for summons are reduced by 1 when calculating AR. Can be leveled up multiple times, each level reducing duration costs by an additional 1.

All Condition, No Strain

Section titled “All Condition, No Strain”

When you wound an enemy with a specific damage type, you may forgo the damage to refund Strain you spent on the roll.

Item Proficiency

Section titled “Item Proficiency”

Choose a specific item or toolset. You gain a die (starting at d12) when using that item for relevant tasks. Each level upgrades the die by one step, up to a maximum of d4.

Items in the same family (e.g. longbow and shortbow) benefit from the proficiency, but the die is one step worse than what the proficiency would normally provide.

Can be taken multiple times for different items or toolsets.

Purchasing Passives

Section titled “Purchasing Passives”

Passives are purchased with XP during downtime or between sessions, with GM approval. Each level of a passive is purchased separately and stacks.

There is no cap on the number of passive abilities a character can have.
