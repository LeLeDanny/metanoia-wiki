Why You Pursued Ren at the Start

Section titled “Why You Pursued Ren at the Start”

Sensing Ren is involuntary. Pursuing it is a choice, and rarely an easy one. Roll a d12.

d12OriginObjects Chased
1You were going to die if you didn’tSelf (Survival), Freedom, Self (Actualization)
2Someone you loved (or yourself) was harmedVengeance, Bonds, Justice
3You were powerless and humiliatedSelf (Ego), Vengeance, Justice
4A mentor chose you/ you were forced to trainBonds, Order, God
5You needed to learn to control it or it could hurt someoneSelf (Actualization), Justice, Order, Balance
6You needed to escape controlFreedom, Self (Autonomy)
7Others depended on youBonds, Peace
8Religious revelation or callingGod, Truth
9You wanted recognitionSelf (Ego), Bonds
10You needed to fix something brokenBalance, Peace, Order, Self (Autonomy), Justice
11It was something expected of youOrder, Bonds, God
12You pursued it for the experience and it was funFreedom, Self (Actualization)

How You Learned Ren

Section titled “How You Learned Ren”

Once you had a reason, someone or something had to teach you. Roll d20.

d20TeacherDescription
1-8Faction cohort based TrainingOutcome-focused training. You were shaped to fulfill a role efficiently as part of a cohort.
9-12Military or Government TrainingStandardized Drills, Battlefield application, discipline under command
13-16Direct Apprenticeship with a Master or Specialized trainingSlow correction OR High-performance environment.
17-19Religious discipline or Academic studyMeditation, doctrine, ritual purification, Theory before Execution
20Forbidden / Stolen / Found TextsKnowledge without guidance
